# Brake system — typical repair cost ranges (India, general reference)

## Brake pad replacement (front or rear, per axle)
Typical range: INR 1,500 - 4,500 for pads alone on most hatchbacks and
sedans; INR 4,000 - 9,000 for SUVs or premium brands. Labor is usually a
small fraction of this if pads are the only work done, since pad
replacement is a routine, low-labor-time job.

## Brake pad + rotor/disc replacement
When pads have worn far enough to score the rotor, expect INR 4,000 -
12,000 per axle depending on vehicle segment, since rotors are a
significant parts cost on top of pads.

## Brake fluid flush
Typical range: INR 800 - 2,000. This is a routine maintenance item, not
an emergency repair, and is usually recommended every 2 years regardless
of symptoms.

## Notes on urgency
Worn brake pads below manufacturer-specified thickness are a safety-
relevant finding. Delaying replacement significantly increases stopping
distance and risks costly rotor damage, which turns a cheaper pad-only
job into a more expensive pad+rotor job.
