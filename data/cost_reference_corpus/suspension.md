# Suspension system — typical repair cost ranges (India, general reference)

## Control arm bush replacement (per side)
Typical range: INR 1,200 - 3,500 for the bush/bushing itself plus labor;
INR 3,000 - 7,000 if the full control arm needs replacing rather than
just the bush. A cracked or perished bush is a common wear item,
especially on vehicles regularly driven on rough roads.

## Shock absorber / strut replacement (per pair)
Typical range: INR 4,000 - 12,000 depending on vehicle segment.

## Notes on urgency
A cracked control arm bush is usually not an immediate safety emergency
on its own, but it does affect handling precision and accelerates wear
on nearby components (tyres, other suspension bushes) if left too long.
Reasonable to schedule within a few weeks rather than same-day, absent
other symptoms like clunking noises or visible play in the wheel.
